Is this you

Six things this program is actually built for

Not weight. Not “bouncing back”. These.

Sleep

You wake at 3am, drenched

And then you lie there until five. It is the most common complaint in this stage, and it is usually the first thing to move.

Heat

The flush arrives in public

In a meeting, at the stove, at a wedding. Cooling breath is a tool you can use sitting down, with nobody noticing.

Joints

Shoulder, knees, hips

Frozen shoulder in your late forties is not a coincidence. Falling oestrogen changes connective tissue. Nobody tells you that either.

Pelvic floor

You leak when you sneeze

Or laugh, or lift a grandchild. We will talk about it plainly, once, and then train it properly — including the release, which is the part most people miss.

Mood

You snapped, and it wasn't you

Irritability, anxiety, the fog that loses a word mid-sentence and makes you quietly frightened. You are not becoming a difficult person.

Bones

You've seen your mother's spine

Bone density falls fastest in the years around the final period. Loading it is the single most useful thing you can do in this decade.

Questions

The things everyone asks

I have never done yoga. Is that a problem?

No. Batch 01 is built for beginners in this specific stage of life. The first class starts with a chair, and the chair stays available for every class after it.

My knees are bad. Can I still do this?

Yes. Every sequence has a chair and wall version, including for knee or hip replacements. You will be asked about this on the health form before you start, and the modifications are planned for you, not improvised.

7am doesn't work for me.

Every class is recorded and posted within three hours, and the homework is designed to be done alone. If enough people in a batch need a second slot, a second slot opens.

Will this help me lose weight?

That is not what this is for, and you will not be weighed. It is built to improve sleep, strength, balance and symptom load — which are measured. If weight loss is your goal, this is honestly the wrong program.

What do I need to buy?

Nothing. A sturdy dining chair, some wall space, two cushions, a dupatta or towel, and two one-litre water bottles. All of it is already in your house.

Can I cancel?

It is month to month. Cancel any time, and there is a seven-day no-questions refund on your first month.

Is this a treatment for menopause?

No, and we will never say otherwise. This is exercise and breath training that helps you manage symptoms and build strength. It does not diagnose, treat or cure anything, and it does not replace your doctor.

Please see a doctor, not a yoga teacher

If you have bleeding between periods, any bleeding twelve months after your last period, chest pain, a sudden severe headache, or calf swelling — that is a medical appointment, and it is more urgent than this class.

Is this you

Six things this is built for

Not weight. Not “bouncing back”. These.

  1. You wake at 3am, drenched

    And then you lie there until five. It is the most common complaint at this stage of life, and it is usually the first thing to move.

  2. The flush arrives in public

    In a meeting, at the stove, at a wedding. Cooling breath is a tool you can use sitting down, with nobody noticing.

  3. Shoulder, knees, hips

    A frozen shoulder in your late forties is not a coincidence. Falling oestrogen changes connective tissue. Nobody tells you that either.

  4. You leak when you sneeze

    Or laugh, or lift a grandchild. We say it plainly once, then train it properly — including the release, which is the part almost everyone misses.

  5. You snapped, and it wasn't you

    Irritability, anxiety, the fog that loses a word mid-sentence and leaves you quietly frightened. You are not becoming a difficult person.

  6. You've seen your mother's spine

    Bone density falls fastest in the years around the final period. Loading it is the most useful thing you can do in this decade.

It's just age. There's nothing to be done.

Questions

The things everyone asks

I have never done yoga. Is that a problem?

No. Batch 01 is built for beginners at this specific stage of life. The first class starts with a chair, and the chair stays available for every class after it.

My knees are bad. Can I still do this?

Yes. Every sequence has a chair and wall version, including for knee or hip replacements. You are asked about this on the health form before you start, so the modifications are planned for you rather than improvised.

Seven in the morning doesn't work for me.

Every class is recorded and posted within three hours, and the homework is designed to be done alone. If enough people in a batch need a second slot, a second slot opens.

Will this help me lose weight?

That is not what this is for, and you will not be weighed. It is built to improve sleep, strength, balance and symptom load — all of which are measured. If weight loss is your goal, this is honestly the wrong program.

What do I need to buy?

Nothing. A sturdy dining chair, some wall space, two cushions, a dupatta or towel, and two one-litre water bottles. All of it is already in your house.

Can I cancel?

It is month to month. Cancel any time, and there is a seven-day no-questions refund on your first month.

Is this a treatment for menopause?

No, and we will never say otherwise. This is exercise and breath training that helps you manage symptoms and build strength. It does not diagnose, treat or cure anything, and it does not replace your doctor.

Please see a doctor, not a yoga teacher

If you have bleeding between periods, any bleeding twelve months after your last period, chest pain, a sudden severe headache, or calf swelling — that is a medical appointment, and it is more urgent than this class.

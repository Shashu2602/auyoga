What it does

Six things
you will feel

Not in a year. Most people notice the first two inside a month.

  1. 01

    You sleep better

    Every class ends with slow breathing and a few quiet minutes. This is the change most people notice first — usually in the second week.

  2. 02

    Your back stops nagging

    Long hours at a desk, or standing in a kitchen, pull the back tight. We open it up slowly, a little every day.

  3. 03

    Stairs get easy again

    Simple standing work makes your legs and knees stronger. After a few weeks the stairs stop feeling like a job.

  4. 04

    Your head gets quieter

    Ten minutes of breathing work does more for a busy mind than an hour of scrolling. You can feel it the same day.

  5. 05

    You get more bendy — slowly

    Week by week, not day one. Nobody here will ask you to touch your toes before your body is ready.

  6. 06

    You have more left in the tank

    A morning class sets up the whole day. Most people say they reach the evening with more in them than before.

Questions

Things people ask first

I have never done yoga. Can I still join?

Yes — most of the batch started that way. The first class begins with a chair, and the chair stays there for every class after it.

My knees and back hurt. Is that a problem?

No. Every pose has a chair or wall version. You tell Anu about it on the short call before your first class, so she plans around it instead of guessing in the middle of a session.

Which batch should I pick?

Take 8–9 if you want it finished before work or school. Take 11–12 if your morning is busy at home — that batch is mostly mothers, and it starts gently.

What if I miss a class?

Every class is recorded and sent out the same day. You can also simply join the other batch that day.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. A mat helps, but a folded bedsheet works. You will want a sturdy chair, a bit of wall, a towel or dupatta, and two water bottles. All of it is in your house already.

Can I try before paying?

Yes. The first class is free. Come, do it, and decide after.

Will I lose weight?

Maybe a little, but that is not what this is for, and you will never be weighed here. This is for sleep, strength, balance and a calmer head.

Is it in Hindi or English?

Both, mixed — whatever the batch is comfortable with. Nobody is left guessing what a pose means.

One honest note

This is exercise and breathing practice, not medical treatment. If you have chest pain, dizziness, very high blood pressure, a recent surgery, or an injury you are unsure about, please talk to your doctor before you start.
